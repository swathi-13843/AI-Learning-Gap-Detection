Instructional Design & Diagnostic Recovery Methodology

The 6-step pathway implements a cognitive scaffolding architecture. Learning begins with low-cognitive-load conceptual reinforcement (theory and diagrams), bridges into applied procedural reasoning (worked examples and multi-modal videos), and terminates in active diagnostic assessment. Once all stages are marked completed and the adaptive validation quiz is submitted, the Bayesian Knowledge Tracing engine recalculates the topic's latent mastery state \(P(L_t)\), measuring the empirical \(\Delta\text{Mastery}\) gain.
