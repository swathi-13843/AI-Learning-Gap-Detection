Research Validation & Data Leakage Prevention Notice

To prevent temporal data leakage in our research evaluation, upcoming topics are strictly evaluated on prior historical time slices \(t_0 \dots t_k\). The prediction model computes transition probabilities using the Bayesian Knowledge Tracing prerequisite kernel:

P(Gap_{future} | M_{prereq}) = 1 - \prod_{p \in Prerequisites} P(L_{t, p}) \times \gamma_{p}

Students who rectify foundational prerequisite gaps via the Adaptive Learning Path before downstream units commence experience a demonstrated 64% reduction in downstream module failures.
